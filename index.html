FAQ

Questions, answered.

What happens in the first week?
Day 1 covers onboarding: you fill one short form, we set up a Frame.io and Notion workspace for your client, receive footage and scripts, and lock the brand style — colors, fonts and caption style. Your first reel lands on day 2-3 as a style sample.
How do you deliver 24 reels that fast?
The work is split three ways: one editor cuts, another adds motion graphics and one more SFX, all running in parallel. Our AI workflow produces 5-8 reels a day, compared with 2-3 from a traditional editor.
How do revisions work?
Your client comments directly on the timeline in Frame.io, so nothing gets lost in a WhatsApp thread. Revisions come back within 48 hours, on days 9-11 of the month.
What if the style is wrong?
That is what the day 2-3 sample is for. You get one finished reel before the batch runs, and your feedback calibrates the remaining 23. Nothing goes into full production until the style is signed off.
Do you edit under my agency’s brand?
Always. Everything ships under your brand, in your client’s own Frame.io and Notion workspace. We never appear in front of your client.
